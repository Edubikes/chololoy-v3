Black Bean Soup

Blend, heat for five minutes, garnish. A velvety soup built on a single tablespoon.

Blend, heat for five minutes, garnish. A velvety soup built on a single tablespoon.

Ingredients

  • ⅔ cup cooked and drained black beans
  • ⅓ cup water
  • 1 tablespoon Chololoy® Original Seasoning

Garnish

  • ½ teaspoon chopped cilantro
  • 1 teaspoon chopped white or red onion
  • 1 teaspoon crumbled bacon
  • 1 teaspoon cooked chorizo
  • 1–2 teaspoons sour cream
  • 4 thin avocado slices

Preparation

  1. In a blender, purée the beans, water and Chololoy® Original Seasoning.
  2. Cook on medium heat for five minutes, stirring often.
  3. Garnish and serve.

• Chololoy® is made only with natural ingredients and no preservatives.

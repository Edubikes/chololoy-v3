Chololoy Spanish Garlic Chicken

Golden chicken cubes in a rich garlic and vinegar sauce, Spanish style. Simple, homestyle and full of character.

Golden chicken cubes in a rich garlic and vinegar sauce, Spanish style. Simple, homestyle and full of character.

Ingredients

The chicken

  • 1 tablespoon Chololoy® Original Seasoning
  • 10 oz chicken breast, cubed
  • A splash of olive oil
  • Salt, to taste

The garlic sauce

  • 3 tablespoons minced garlic
  • ½ cup white vinegar

To serve

  • White rice, to taste

Preparation

  1. Season: Coat the chicken cubes thoroughly with Chololoy® Original Seasoning.
  2. Brown: In a hot skillet with olive oil, cook the chicken until golden on all sides, then set aside.
  3. The garlic: In the same skillet, add the minced garlic and let it turn lightly golden without burning.
  4. The vinegar: Pour in the vinegar and let it boil briefly so it reduces into a rich sauce.
  5. Finish: Return the chicken, season with salt, and simmer on low heat about 10 minutes. Serve over white rice.
Mom’s secret: Browning the chicken first keeps it juicy. The reduced vinegar and garlic sauce is so good you’ll want this again and again.

Suggested pairing: fluffy white rice and a dry white wine, or lemonade.
